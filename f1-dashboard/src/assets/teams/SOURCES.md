# Team logo sources

The 11 SVG files in this directory were downloaded on 15 October 2026 from the assets used by the [official Formula 1 teams page](https://www.formula1.com/en/teams).
These are the standard logo assets, including the original colors for Ferrari, McLaren, Red Bull, Alpine, Williams, Racing Bulls and Haas. The four black-only marks have local fill overrides for visibility on the dark dashboard: Mercedes silver (`#C0C6CF`), Aston Martin green (`#229971`), Audi red (`#FF2D00`) and Cadillac silver (`#AAAAAD`). Their shapes are unchanged.
